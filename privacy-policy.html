Fumblish Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Fumblish ("the app") is published by Routapolku (Juho Vasenius, Finland). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: your learning data stays on your device, and the text you write in conversations is sent to an AI provider to generate answers.

Data stored on your device

Word lists, practice history, conversations, summaries, evaluations and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android's backup service may include this data in your Google backup according to your device settings.

Data sent to an AI provider

To generate replies, summaries, evaluations, explanations and example sentences, the app sends the relevant text to a large-language-model provider (currently OpenAI). This includes the messages you write in a conversation, the words in the word list you are working on, and the built-in situation descriptions. No name, email address or device identifier is attached to these requests. The provider processes the text to produce the answer and handles it under its own API data policy, which does not use API data for model training. Do not write personal information about yourself or others into conversations.

Subscription and payments

Premium is sold as a Google Play subscription. Google handles the purchase, payment details and renewal. The app only receives a purchase token to check whether the subscription is active. We do not receive your payment details.

Optional diagnostic log

The app can keep a local log of AI requests and responses to help you check a result. It is stored only on your device, is off by default in the released app, and you can share or delete it from Settings.

Data we do not collect

The app has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If the app starts sending data to our own servers in a future version (for example, to sync your word lists between devices), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
